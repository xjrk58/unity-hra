## The project

scripts (character, monster, railing, column, lamp, goal), the app icon, the
synthesised sounds and this documentation are released under the
**MIT licence** – see [`LICENSE`](../../../LICENSE).

The files listed below come from other people and keep their own licences.

### What CC BY 3.0 asks for

The recordings may be used, changed and passed on, also commercially, as long
as the author is credited and changes are indicated. The project does this in
three places; keep them when you redistribute the game or the clips:

- `Assets/Resources/Audio/Farts/ATTRIBUTION.txt` – source, licence, what was changed;
- the end-of-match banner in the game – one line of credit;
- this page and the README.

Changes made to the recording: cut into individual clips, mixed to mono,
high-pass filtered, faded, loudness-normalised (`Tools/assets/slice_farts.py`).

If you would rather not carry the attribution, delete the clips: the game then
synthesises its fart sounds.

## Not in the repository

- **Unity** itself and the Universal Render Pipeline package are downloaded by
  Unity Hub and are covered by Unity's own terms. A built game contains Unity's
  runtime.
- The **source files** the textures, sky and props were made from are fetched
  by `Tools/assets/fetch.py`; only the processed results are in the repository.

## Fonts

The game uses Unity's built-in font resource and, on phones, the system font.
No font files are in the repository.

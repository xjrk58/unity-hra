# Prdivý závod (Fart Race) – documentation

Prdivý závod is a small first-person game made with Unity 6. Three (or four)
competitors run around a four-storey car park, collect floating green farts
into a backpack and expel them at the goal; whoever delivers 15 first wins.
About 45 seconds in, a fart monster turns the day into a green gloom and starts
hunting. The game's own texts are in Czech; these pages quote them as they
appear on screen.

The documentation is organised the [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr) way. Pick
the part that matches what you need right now.

# Tutorial: your first match

In this tutorial you will play one complete match on a computer: collect farts,
deliver them, blast a rival, survive the monster and read the final ranking. It
takes about five minutes. You need a built copy of the game (see
[Build and run from source](02-build-and-run-from-source.md) if you do not have
one yet).

## 1. Start the game

Start `PrdivyZavod` (`PrdivyZavod.x86_64` on Linux, `PrdivyZavod.exe` on
Windows, `PrdivyZavod.app` on a Mac). The title screen shows the car park from
above and three buttons.

Click **Hrát sám** ("play alone").

You are now standing on the ground deck of the car park. Top left is the
scoreboard: **Ty** (you), **Pšouk** (red) and **Bzdina** (blue). Bottom left is
your backpack, **Batůžek 0 / 8**.

## 2. Collect farts

Look around with the mouse. The glowing green clouds with a beam of light above
them are farts. Walk into one with `W` `A` `S` `D`.

The backpack counter goes up to 1 and one of its eight slots lights up. Collect
a few more. Hold `Shift` while walking forward to sprint.

## 3. Deliver them

As soon as you carry something, an orange marker **CÍL** ("goal") with a
distance appears; it sticks to the edge of the screen when the goal is behind
you. The goal is the orange extraction hood in a corner of the ground deck.

Walk under the hood and **hold the left mouse button**. One fart leaves the
backpack every moment and your score rises. The first to reach 15 wins.

## 4. Blast a rival

Collect at least two farts, then put the small white square in the middle of
the screen on a bot. The square turns orange and a label says
**… – prdni po něm!** Click the **right mouse button**.

The bot disappears in a green cloud for ten seconds and the scoreboard shows
**vyřazen** next to its name. The blast cost you two farts.

## 5. Meet the monster

About 38 seconds into the match the lamps begin to flicker and
**Něco se blíží…** ("something is coming") appears. At 45 seconds it gets dark
and **PRDOVÁ PŘÍŠERA!** bursts out of the floor.

- If the top of the screen says **PŘÍŠERA TĚ LOVÍ!** it is after you. Sprint –
  walking is not fast enough.
- Or turn round, aim at it and click the right mouse button (two farts): it
  shrieks and stays away for a while.
- If it catches you, you lose the whole backpack and are out for five seconds.

It comes back several times until the match ends.

## 6. Finish

When somebody reaches 15, a banner shows the winner and **Konečné pořadí**
("final ranking") with everybody's score. Press `R` for a new game or `Esc` to
quit.
